DIGITAL POLTHEL PAHANA - CLIENT QUICK GUIDE
============================================

No installation, Node.js, or npm is required.

EVENT DAY
---------
1. Connect the Windows laptop and tablet to the same private Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
2. Double-click Digital-Polthel-Pahana.exe.
3. If Windows Firewall asks, tick "Private networks" and click "Allow access".
4. Keep the black Digital Polthel Pahana window open throughout the ceremony.
5. The Setup page opens automatically in the laptop browser.
6. Click "Open Main Display" and then "Begin Ceremony Display" for the projector.
7. Scan the QR code on the Setup page using the tablet camera.
8. Use the tablet controller to light the 15 pahan.

TO FINISH
---------
Close the black Digital Polthel Pahana window after the event.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Guest/university Wi-Fi can block communication between devices. A private router
  or phone hotspot is recommended.
- The first launch may show a Windows SmartScreen warning because this event app is
  not commercially code-signed. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
- Do not close the black app window while the ceremony is running.
- If the tablet cannot connect, confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi and that
  the Windows Firewall prompt was allowed for Private networks.
